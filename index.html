Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

Not at all. Most clients leave a key, code or lockbox. You’ll get a text when we arrive and another when your home is finished.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes — products, microfiber cloths and equipment are all included. We use family- and pet-safe products, and we’ll use yours if you prefer.

How much does a cleaning cost?

It depends on the size of the home, its condition and how often we come. Call or text for a flat-rate quote with no hidden fees.

Are you insured?

Yes. We’re fully insured and every clean is backed by our 24-hour satisfaction guarantee — if something was missed, we come back and make it right at no charge.

Do you clean apartments and Airbnbs?

We clean apartments, condos, houses, short-term rentals and small offices. Airbnb turnovers include laundry, restocking and a photo checklist.

How do I pay?

Cash, card, Zelle or Venmo — whatever is easiest. Payment is due the day of service, and recurring clients can keep a card on file.
